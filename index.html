Built by a maker with a Bambu Lab X2D and a growing list of orders, for anyone who has outgrown the spreadsheet.

Orders, structured

Every job moves through Quote → Queued → Printing → Done → Delivered. See what is due, what is unpaid and what is next at a glance.

True cost per print

Filament by the gram, electricity by the kWh, machine wear by the hour, labour and a failure buffer. Get a suggested price before you quote.

Printables & MakerWorld

Paste a model link and 3D Brains fills in the title, preview and source, so you always know where a design came from.

Drop a sliced file

Drag in a .gcode.3mf from Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer, or a PrusaSlicer .gcode. Print time, grams and colours fill in automatically.

Filament that tracks itself

Spools are deducted when an order is done. Low-stock spools surface on your dashboard before you run out mid-print.

Know what you earn

Revenue, real profit, margin, hours on the printer and kilograms used, by month. Export everything to CSV whenever you need it.
